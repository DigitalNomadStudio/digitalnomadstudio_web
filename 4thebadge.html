Web App

4thebadge

For the fans who live for the badge

Your club. Always with you.

4thebadge is a football app that becomes your club's app. Pick your league and your club, and everything - the badge, the colours, the home screen icon - takes on your side's identity. From then on it is your club's live scores, fixtures, results, league table, squad and stats, in one place and a tap away.

It is built for supporters who follow a club rather than a league: the League Two devotee, the A-League regular, the expat keeping up with the Championship from Sydney. Because it runs as a progressive web app there is nothing to download from an App Store. Open it in your browser, add it to your home screen, and it works like a native app on iPhone and Android.

How it works

1

Pick your club

Choose your league and search for your club - from the Premier League to League Two, the Scottish Premiership, the A-League and Europe's top divisions.

2

It becomes your club's app

The badge, theme and home screen icon switch to your club's colours, and your club is highlighted wherever it appears - the table, the fixtures, the Golden Boot race.

3

Add it to your home screen

No App Store and no download queue. Add 4thebadge to your home screen from Safari or Chrome and it opens like any other app.

4

Live on matchday

Scores update every minute while the match is on, with every goal, card and substitution as it happens. Between matches, the next fixture and latest result are always up front.

Everything a fan needs

  • Live scores - updated every minute during matches, so you are never a refresh behind
  • Match centre - scorers and assists, cards and substitutions, formations with player ratings, and the match stats side by side
  • Fixtures and results - the next five fixtures in your local time and every result of the season, with form at a glance
  • League table - live standings with form and goal difference, your club picked out wherever it sits
  • The squad - the full first-team squad by position, with season stats a tap away
  • Golden Boot - top scorers and assists across each competition, updated as the goals go in
  • My Leagues - follow whole competitions, with matchweeks, results and the top and bottom of every table, Champions League included
  • Up to five clubs - the local side, the boyhood club and the one you picked up on holiday, each in its own colours

Competitions covered

Twelve domestic leagues you can follow club by club, plus the three UEFA club competitions in My Leagues.

Free to start, Pro when you want more

Every new supporter gets 30 days of Pro free, with no card and no sign-up needed. After that, Free keeps your club close and Pro brings the whole season to life.

  • Free - one club from any of the 12 domestic leagues, next match and last result, league table and season stats, and the full squad
  • Pro - A$5 a month or A$50 a year: live scores every minute, full match detail, every fixture and result of the season, the Golden Boot race and head-to-head records, My Leagues across all 15 competitions, and up to five clubs

Accessible by design

4thebadge is built to work for every fan, including supporters with a visual impairment. It works fully with VoiceOver on iPhone and TalkBack on Android, so every score, event and stat can be read aloud. The dark theme with gold accents meets WCAG contrast standards, text scales with your device settings, and every control can be reached from a keyboard or switch device.

Built with

4thebadge is a progressive web app: one codebase serving iPhone, Android and desktop browsers, installable without an app store, with match data synced live into a cloud database while play is under way.
