Reefglass

A living coral reef for iPhone and iPhone Duo.

A Reef That Lives on Your iPhone

Reefglass is a coral reef aquarium for iPhone, built from the ground up for the folding iPhone Duo. Nearly forty fish from more than twenty-five reef species school, hover, dart and drift through rock, seaweed and coral, lit by shafts of sunlight and dappled caustics on the sand.

Nothing is a video and nothing loops. Every fish is simulated: they flock, keep out of each other's way and steer round the rocks. The clownfish dart into their anemones and peek back out, and every few minutes a sea turtle swims in, circles the reef and is gone again.

Made for the iPhone Duo

  • One tank, two screens - unfold for the full panoramic reef on the inner display; fold it and the same tank re-frames to the cover display. The fish never reset
  • Tent it - it becomes a bedside aquarium with the time, and the screen stays awake
  • Fold it - the water sloshes with the hinge

On any other iPhone it is the same living reef on a single screen, in portrait or landscape, with the clock whenever you want it.

The Reef Keeps Time

The light follows the real day: full sun through the afternoon, a warm dim at dusk and moonlight overnight. The fish settle low and slow when it is dark, and the morning brings them back up.

  • Keep the screen on and the tank becomes a night light that dims to a deeper moonlight and slows down to go easy on the battery - only while charging, unless you say otherwise
  • Let the water sounds carry on after the screen locks, with a sleep timer of 30 minutes, an hour, two hours or until you come back
  • Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets show the reef with the time
  • An "Open Reefglass" Shortcuts action pairs with a charger automation, so the tank comes on when you dock the phone

It's Your Tank

  • Five reefs - tropical reef, coral garden, coral pinnacle, sea fan garden and sponge terrace - and your fish come with you when you switch
  • Choose your stock: more tangs, fewer butterflyfish, as many clownfish as you like and up to two turtles
  • Colour the anemones fourteen ways, from the reef's own to ruby, teal and gold
  • Double tap the glass and the fish startle; pinch to look closer at any corner of the reef
  • Set the pace - slow the fish right down for a calmer tank
  • Mix the sound: underwater ambience, air-stone bubbles and filter hum, each on its own slider
  • Three calm ambient music pieces, over the water or instead of it
  • Rendered live in Metal, easing off by itself when the phone is warm or in Low Power Mode

One Price, Nothing Else to Buy

Reefglass will be a single one-time purchase on the App Store. Every reef, every fish and every setting is included.

There are no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads and no account to create.

Nothing Collected, Nothing Tracked

Reefglass does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your settings stay on your phone, the app asks for no permissions and it makes no network requests. There are no third-party SDKs, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing leaves your phone. See our Privacy Policy for the detail.

The fish, the turtle and the coral are hand-modelled and photo-textured, and every reef texture and sound is public domain (CC0), credited in the app.
