AI automation, custom agents and rapid prototyping for SMEs - designed and built in Sydney by the team behind TeamRelay

Practical AI, Built to Ship

Digital Nomad Studio is a Sydney-based software studio. We work with small and medium businesses across Australia to put AI to work on real problems: the admin that eats up staff time, the enquiries that wait too long for an answer, the products that need smarter features, and the ideas that deserve a working prototype before anyone commits to a full build.

We are builders first. TeamRelay, our app for multilingual teams and families, runs real-time speech translation entirely on-device with no internet connection, and our founders have spent recent years developing AI-based solutions that enhance decision-making and automate complex workflows. That experience shapes every engagement: start small, prove it on your real data, then build properly.

AI Rapid Prototyping

The fastest way to find out what AI can do for your business is to see it working on your own data. Our rapid prototyping engagement takes one clearly defined problem - a quoting process, an overflowing inbox, a pile of documents, a product idea - and turns it into a working prototype in two to three weeks. You see real results, we both learn what it takes to run it properly, and you decide about a full build with evidence instead of a slide deck.

What you get

  • A short discovery session to agree the one question the prototype must answer
  • A working prototype built on your real data and real examples, not mock-ups
  • Honest measurement of quality, cost and speed, and what it would take to run day to day
  • A clear recommendation and roadmap - build, adjust or stop - with no obligation to continue

AI Automation & Custom Agents

Most small and medium businesses have work that is repetitive, rules-based and still done by hand: quoting and invoicing, triaging inboxes, reading and summarising documents, re-keying data between systems, drafting routine responses. We build AI automation and agents that take on that work reliably, connect to the tools you already use, and hand over to a person when judgement is needed.

Typical projects

  • Quote and invoice preparation - from a voice note, photo or email to a draft ready to send
  • Document intake and extraction - read forms, reports, and emails, pull out what matters, and file it where it belongs
  • Inbox and request triage - classify, prioritise, and route incoming work with suggested responses
  • Back-office automation - connect the systems you already use so data moves without re-keying
  • Knowledge assistants - answer staff or customer questions from your own documents and policies
  • Monitoring and alerts - watch data sources and flag the exceptions that need a human decision

AI Features in Existing Products

You do not need to rebuild your product to make it smarter. We add well-scoped AI capabilities to software you already run - web, mobile, or internal tools - and integrate them cleanly with your existing data, sign-in, and design. Where privacy or connectivity matters, we can run models on-device or inside your own environment, as we do in TeamRelay.

What we add

  • Natural-language search and question answering over your own content
  • Summarisation of long documents, threads, and reports
  • Classification, tagging, and smart routing
  • Real-time speech-to-text and translation
  • Recommendations and personalisation based on how people actually use your product
  • In-app assistants that guide users through your existing workflows

How We Work

The same staged approach we use for the apps we publish: a rapid prototype first, then a full build only if the results warrant it. You see progress early and make decisions with confidence.

1

Discover

We map the workflow, the data, and what a good result looks like, and agree a clear scope for the first stage.

2

Prototype

A working version on your real data, usually within two to three weeks, so you see what the technology actually does for you before building more.

3

Build

We integrate with your systems, add the guardrails and testing it needs, and prepare it for everyday use.

4

Run and Improve

We launch it, watch how it performs, and refine it as your needs change. Support stays with the people who built it.
