Practical AI, Built to Ship

Digital Nomad Studio is an app studio and an AI agency. Alongside the apps we publish, we work directly with businesses to put AI to work on real problems: repetitive processes that eat up staff time, products that need smarter features, and ideas that deserve a working prototype before anyone commits to a full build.

Custom AI Agents & Automation

Most businesses have work that is repetitive, rules-based, and still done by hand: triaging inboxes, reading and summarising documents, re-keying data between systems, drafting routine responses. We build agents and automated workflows that take on that work reliably, with clear hand-offs to a person when judgement is needed.

Typical projects

  • Document intake and extraction - read forms, reports, and emails, pull out what matters, and file it where it belongs
  • Inbox and request triage - classify, prioritise, and route incoming work with suggested responses
  • Back-office automation - connect the systems you already use so data moves without re-keying
  • Knowledge assistants - answer staff or customer questions from your own documents and policies
  • Monitoring and alerts - watch data sources and flag the exceptions that need a human decision

AI Features in Existing Products

You do not need to rebuild your product to make it smarter. We add well-scoped AI capabilities to software you already run - web, mobile, or internal tools - and integrate them cleanly with your existing data, sign-in, and design. Where privacy or connectivity matters, we can run models on-device or inside your own environment, as we do in TeamRelay.

What we add

  • Natural-language search and question answering over your own content
  • Summarisation of long documents, threads, and reports
  • Classification, tagging, and smart routing
  • Real-time speech-to-text and translation
  • Recommendations and personalisation based on how people actually use your product
  • In-app assistants that guide users through your existing workflows

AI-Powered MVPs & Prototypes

Have an idea for an AI product? The fastest way to find out whether it works is to build the smallest version that can be put in front of real users. We scope an MVP around the one thing it must do well, build it on a foundation that can grow, and help you test it with the people it is meant for, so investment decisions are made on evidence rather than guesswork.

What you get

  • A short discovery to define the core question the prototype must answer
  • A working prototype built on your real data and use cases, not mock-ups
  • Honest measurement of quality, cost, and speed before you scale
  • A clear path from prototype to production build if the results warrant it

How We Work

The same staged approach we use for the apps we publish: prototype first, then full build. You see progress early and make decisions with confidence.

1

Discover

We map the workflow, the data, and what a good result looks like, and agree a clear scope for the first stage.

2

Prototype

A working version on your real data, so you see what the technology actually does for you before building more.

3

Build

We integrate with your systems, add the guardrails and testing it needs, and prepare it for everyday use.

4

Run and Improve

We launch it, watch how it performs, and refine it as your needs change. Support stays with the people who built it.
