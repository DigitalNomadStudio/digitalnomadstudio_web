AI for Your Business

Working out where AI can genuinely help your business, and where it cannot

Start With the Business, Not the Technology

Most businesses we talk to are not short of AI tools to try. What they are short of is a clear answer to a simpler question: where would it actually help us? That is the conversation we are here for.

We are a software studio, so we come at it practically: what does your team spend its time on, what would be worth changing, and is AI the right tool for that or not. We keep an open mind about the answer.

How We Can Help

  • A conversation first - we look at how your business runs today and where the time goes
  • A short list of candidates - the places AI looks worth trying, and the ones it does not
  • Try one small thing - if something stands out, we can build a small prototype on your own examples so you can judge it for yourself
  • A plain recommendation - carry on, change direction or stop, with no obligation either way

If the answer turns out to be an app or a product of its own, that is the core of what we do. Our Software Development page covers it.

Wondering Where to Start?

Tell us a little about your business and what is taking up your team's time. Marco, our AI assistant, can help you describe it, or use the project form. Either way, we'll get back to you within two business days for a no-obligation conversation.
